Blip's Big Adventure — a cute 2D platformer game

Walk with the arrow keys, hold Shift to run, and press Space or the Up arrow to jump — press it again in mid-air for a double jump. Collect stars, avoid the red spike hazards, and reach the flag at the end of each of the 10 stages. On a touch screen, use the on-screen arrow, run, and jump buttons instead.
